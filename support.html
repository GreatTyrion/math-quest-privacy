Getting started

  1. On first launch, type a name for each child, pick their grade and a picture. Setup starts with two players; tap Remove on one you don't need, or Add another player for up to four.
  2. Choose a 4-digit parent PIN and tap Start playing!
  3. Each child taps their own picture to play.

Which games match my child's class?

Each grade follows the PEP (人教版) primary maths textbook for that term, one game per unit. A child sees their own grade first and can peek at the other grades from their home screen. A parent can change a child's grade in the parent zone.

Can we use it with the textbook?

Yes, and that is the best way to use it. On every game's level screen, the textbook unit it practises is shown under the title, so a child can play the game for the unit their class is on, or open the book at that unit when a level is hard. The learn cards explain each skill too, but the textbook is where it is taught in full.

The textbooks are free to read on the Ministry of Education's National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools (国家中小学智慧教育平台): basic.smartedu.cn/tchMaterial. Math Quest is not affiliated with People's Education Press or the platform.

How do I change the language?

Language is chosen inside the app, not by the iPad's settings. In the parent zone, each child has an English / 中文 switch, and the switch at the top changes the language of the parent zone and the player picker.

How do points and prizes work?

Children earn points for right answers and spend them in the prize shop. The prizes are whatever you set up in the parent zone. When a child asks for one, it waits for you: approve it, or return the points.

I forgot the parent PIN

On the PIN screen, tap Forgot the PIN?, hold the button for three seconds, and answer a multiplication question meant for a grown-up. Then choose a new PIN. Every child's progress, points and prizes are kept.

Can I add or remove a child later?

Yes, in the parent zone. + Add a player sits below the children, up to eight in all. Each child's card has a Remove button, which asks first, because removing a child deletes their stars, points, badges and prizes. The last remaining child can't be removed.

Does it sync between devices?

No. Everything is stored on the iPad it was entered on. The app has no account and no server. If your iPad backs up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data is included in that backup like any other app's.

开始使用

  1. 第一次打开时，为每个孩子填写名字，选择年级和头像。设置页一开始有两位玩家； 不需要的可以点删掉，也可以点+ 再加一个人，最多四位。
  2. 设定一个 4 位数字的家长密码，然后点开始玩吧！
  3. 每个孩子点自己的头像开始玩。

哪些游戏和孩子的课程对应？

每个年级都按照该学期的人教版小学数学教材编排，一个单元对应一个游戏。孩子会先看到 自己年级的游戏，也可以在主页上看看其他年级。家长可以在家长区更改孩子的年级。

可以和课本一起用吗？

可以，这也是最好的用法。每个游戏的关卡页面上，标题下面都写着它练习的是课本哪个单元，孩子可以 玩班上正在学的那个单元的游戏；遇到难的关卡，也可以翻开课本的那个单元看一看。知识卡片 也会讲解每个知识点，但最完整的讲解还是在课本里。

课本可以在教育部的国家中小学智慧教育平台免费阅读： basic.smartedu.cn/tchMaterial。 数学大冒险与人民教育出版社及该平台均无关联。

怎么切换语言？

语言在应用里选择，与 iPad 的系统设置无关。在家长区里，每个孩子都有一个 English / 中文 开关；顶部的开关则用来切换家长区和选择玩家页面的语言。

积分和奖品是怎么回事？

孩子答对题目获得积分，可以在奖品店里兑换。奖品由你在家长区设定。孩子申请兑换后， 奖品会等你处理：你可以同意，也可以把积分退回。

忘记了家长密码

在输入密码的页面点忘记密码？，按住按钮 3 秒，再回答一道给家长的 乘法题，然后设定新密码即可。所有孩子的进度、积分和奖品都会保留。

以后还能添加或删除孩子吗？

可以，在家长区里操作。孩子列表下方有+ 添加玩家，最多八位。每个孩子 的卡片上都有删除按钮，会先请你确认，因为删除孩子会一并删除其星星、积分、 徽章和奖品。最后一位孩子不能删除。

会在多台设备之间同步吗？

不会。所有内容都保存在输入它的那台 iPad 上。应用没有账号，也没有服务器。 如果你的 iPad 备份到 iCloud 或电脑，应用的数据会像其他应用一样包含在备份中。
