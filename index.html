Math Quest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Math Quest does not collect any data about you or your children.

The app has no account system, no server, and makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

What the app stores

To work, the app keeps a small database inside its own storage on your iPad:

  • each player's name, picture, grade and language, as entered during setup or in the parent zone;
  • their progress: stars, scores, points and badges;
  • the prizes you set up, and which ones were asked for;
  • the parent PIN, stored only as a one-way hash, never as the digits themselves.

That database never leaves your iPad. Nobody, including the developer, can see it.

What happens to your data

  • It is not transmitted anywhere.
  • It is not shared with anyone.
  • It is not used for advertising or analytics.

If your iPad is set to back up to iCloud or to a computer, the app's database is included in that backup, the same as any other app's data. That backup is controlled entirely by Apple's settings on your device, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPad removes everything it stored. There is nothing else to delete and no account to close.

Children

Math Quest is made for primary-school children and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Player names stay on the iPad they were typed into.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.

本应用没有账号系统，没有服务器，也不发起任何网络请求。应用中没有数据统计、 没有广告、没有追踪，也没有应用内购买。

应用保存了什么

为了正常使用，应用会在 iPad 上属于本应用的存储空间里保存一个小型数据库：

  • 每位玩家的名字、头像、年级和语言，由你在设置时或家长区中填写；
  • 他们的进度：星星、成绩、积分和徽章；
  • 你设定的奖品，以及孩子申请过哪些奖品；
  • 家长密码，只以单向哈希的形式保存，从不保存数字本身。

该数据库不会离开你的 iPad。任何人都无法查看，包括开发者本人。

你的数据会被如何处理

  • 不会被传输到任何地方。
  • 不会与任何人共享。
  • 不会用于广告或数据分析。

如果你的 iPad 设置了 iCloud 备份或电脑备份，本应用的数据库会像其他应用的数据 一样包含在备份中。该备份完全由你设备上的 Apple 设置控制，与本应用无关。

删除你的数据

从 iPad 上删除本应用，即可清除它保存的全部内容。没有其他需要删除的内容， 也没有需要注销的账号。

儿童

数学大冒险是为小学生设计的，不向任何人收集数据，儿童也不例外。玩家的名字只 保存在输入它的那台 iPad 上。
